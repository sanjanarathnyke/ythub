MP4 (video)

resolution = visual detail. higher = bigger file.
144p
Data saver. Watchable on a small screen, smallest file size.
360p
Basic. Fine for phones on a slow connection.
480p
Standard. Decent balance of clarity and size.
720p
HD. Sharp on laptops and most TVs.
1080p
Full HD. The usual default for a crisp picture.
1440p
2K. Noticeably sharper on larger monitors.
2160p
4K. Maximum detail, largest file size.
source
Original. Whatever quality was originally uploaded, untouched.

MP3 (audio)

bitrate = audio fidelity. higher = closer to source.
96kbps
Tiny file. Noticeably compressed, ok for spoken word.
128kbps
Standard. The old default — fine on phone speakers.
192kbps
Good. Clearer on headphones, still compact.
256kbps
High. Hard to tell from the original for most ears.
320kbps
Max MP3. The highest quality the MP3 format supports.
V0
Variable rate. Adjusts bitrate per second — efficient and high quality.
FLAC
Lossless. Bit-for-bit copy of the source, much bigger file.
WAV
Uncompressed. Raw audio, largest file, no processing.
